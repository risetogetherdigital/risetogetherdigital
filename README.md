<h2 align="center">Digital experiences that help businesses grow</h2>

<p align="center">
  <strong>RiseTogether Digital</strong> is a Sri Lankan web design and development brand helping small businesses, startups, entrepreneurs and personal brands build a confident online presence.
</p>

<p align="center">We turn ideas into modern, responsive websites—with thoughtful design, practical features and affordable solutions.</p>

## ✨ What We Do

- 🌐 **Business websites** — A clear, professional home for your business online.
- 💄 **Beauty, salon and spa websites** — Elegant experiences that showcase your services.
- 🍽️ **Restaurant websites** — Modern pages for menus, locations and customer enquiries.
- 💼 **Portfolio websites** — Present your work, services and achievements.
- 🎯 **Landing pages** — Focused pages for campaigns, products and enquiries.
- 📱 **Responsive development** — Smooth, usable layouts across phones, tablets and desktops.
- 🎨 **Modern UI design** — Clean visuals tailored to your brand and audience.
